## Idea entries from the research report (verbatim)

### K2. Persistent contact multipliers on the current projection collider
*Sources: contact note (F1, A2, A3), solver note (idea 9).*
**Outline.** The jitter diagnosis is an inference (projection plus projected line search plus after-the-fact friction gives an inconsistent active set; no source diagnosing this was found). Replace hard projection by an augmented-Lagrangian term with multipliers updated after each converged Newton iteration, so the on/off chatter disappears; K1 is the same mathematics with finite stiffness. The research-grade version is the barrier-free paper (Zheng, Luo, Li, arXiv 2512.12151 v3, 28 May 2026, DOI 10.1145/3811035): a second-order constrained optimisation with a custom augmented Lagrangian, all required contact pairs added immediately to avoid time-of-impact locking, adaptive multipliers, active-set filtering, up to 103x faster than GIPC on GPU; code is on the project page, licence not checked; the page gave no explicit statement about cloth or friction ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.12151), [project](https://simulation-intelligence.github.io/barrier-free)). Multipliers are contact forces, which feeds pressure readouts.
**Assessment.** Payoff high; difficulty days for the cheap variant, weeks to months for the full paper (only the abstract-level view was read, authors' code is GPU-oriented). Risk: lambda converges slowly for tight wedges.


### K3. Friction inside the Newton solve (lagged) and a per-surface mu table
*Sources: contact note (C1, C2).*
**Outline.** Today Coulomb friction is applied after the solve. Move to lagged smoothed friction (freeze normal force and tangent basis from the previous iteration, add the friction potential and its PSD-projected Hessian), standard in IPC and provided by ipc-toolkit ([repo](https://github.com/ipc-sim/ipc-toolkit)). Add a small mu matrix in `fabrics.json` (skin-fabric, fabric-fabric, optional warp/weft anisotropy).
**Assessment.** Payoff high for jitter; difficulty weeks; lagged friction converges only to a fixed point (a few outer iterations). **No verified friction coefficients exist in the notes**: a 2011 study measured 20 woven fabrics against skin under 6 wet conditions but the numbers were paywalled ([JFBI](https://global-sci.com/article/86811/experimental-method-and-characterization-of-friction-properties-between-woven-fabric-and-human-skin-in-wet-condition)); mu must be calibrated or exposed. Stick-slip and velocity-dependent friction matter only for offline motion; deprioritised.
