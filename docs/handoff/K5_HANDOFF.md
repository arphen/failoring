## Idea entries from the research report (verbatim)
Source: "Cloth and crochet simulation ideas.md" (2026-10-13). Payoff, difficulty and cost figures there are the researchers' estimates, not measurements on this code.

### K5. Edge-edge and point-triangle barrier with CCD (IPC-style, CPU)
*Sources: contact note (A1-A4, E4), solver note (idea 9), crochet note (idea 1).*
**Outline.** Add edge-edge candidate pairs to the spatial hash; a smoothly clamped barrier b(d, dhat) per pair with thickness offset; barrier gradient and PSD-projected 12x12 Hessian blocks into the assembled system; a CCD max-step cap (about 0.9 alpha) before each line-search step; adaptive stiffness as in ipc-toolkit. Variants worth reading: Ando's **cubic barrier with elasticity-inclusive dynamic stiffness** (ACM TOG 43(6), 2024; code in ppf-contact-solver, Apache-2.0, but CUDA 12.8 and single precision, so maths only; fabric presets calibrated to real measurements) ([repo](https://github.com/st-tech/ppf-contact-solver), [ACM DL](https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/3687908)); **Geometric Contact Potential** (Huang et al., arXiv 2402.00719, TOG 2025 DOI 10.1145/3731142), a discretisation-independent drop-in for IPC barriers that reduces resolution dependence ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.00719)); **Divide and Truncate** (Chen, Hsu, Ayman, Macklin, arXiv 2604.15513, 16 Apr 2026, venue not stated), a solver-agnostic post-process that truncates displacements to stay inside exclusive regions, no code seen ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15513)). GPU-only alternatives noted: libuipc (Apache-2.0, CUDA 12.8) ([repo](https://github.com/spiriMirror/libuipc)) and Mil2's non-distance barrier (arXiv 2403.19272, no code found) ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.19272)).
**Assessment.** Payoff high (thin tangles, cloth-cloth, wedged garments); difficulty weeks. Risks: contact Hessian blocks change the Cholesky fill pattern each iteration (re-run symbolic factorisation or pre-register pairs; the researchers inference is that this is acceptable in a quasi-static workflow, no timings were found); log-barrier ill-conditioning near dhat; CCD must be conservative. IPC cannot untangle an already-intersecting start. No iteration counts or timings of CPU IPC Newton on garment-scale meshes were found. Also the prerequisite for Y1. Licence: MIT/Apache sources, port rather than link.


### K6. Mesh-based body barrier instead of the SDF
*Sources: contact note (E4).* Run K5's barrier between cloth and the skinned body mesh (kinematic obstacle, linear-trajectory CCD): no bake at all, edge-edge covered. Payoff high; difficulty weeks given K5. Risks: more contact pairs; surface roughness versus the SDF's smoothness (about 7 mm in the note) may need a smoothed offset surface for friction stability. The docs also say the Godot wrapper does not yet expose the mesh collider.


### K7. Layered garments: thickness, ordering, seam exclusion
*Sources: contact note (B1-B3).* Per-panel thickness from `fabrics.json` used as d minus half the summed thicknesses inside the barrier, a layer-index prior with outer layers initialised by radial inflation (IPC needs a clean start), and ignoring the barrier between plies sharing a seam. Thickened codimensional models lock as the mesh is refined; Kaufman's group proposes a mesh-resolution-independent contact model (Gomez-Nogales et al., arXiv 2510.10256, 11 Oct 2025, preprint; the page mentions no layered cloth and no code) ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.10256)). Payoff medium-high (waistband over trousers, sleeve versus torso); difficulty days once K5 exists. No paper on layer-aware garment-over-garment resolution was found for 2023-2026. NVIDIA's Bolt simulates layers progressively bottom-up with lower layers frozen into an SDF, which is a cheap fallback without K5 ([arXiv 2504.17614](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.17614)).
