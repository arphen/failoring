## Idea entries from the research report (verbatim)
Source: "Cloth and crochet simulation ideas.md" (2026-10-13). Payoff, difficulty and cost figures there are the researchers' estimates, not measurements on this code.

### K4. Stop re-baking the SDF every frame
*Sources: contact note (E1, E2), solver note (Lipschitz), docs.*
**Outline.** For a rigid mannequin or rigidly posed limb keep the rest-pose SDF and transform query points by the inverse transform; for per-bone pieces use one SDF per region blended with min or smooth-min at joints; otherwise re-evaluate only grid bricks within about three cloth thicknesses of the garment, lazily with a cache.
**Assessment.** Payoff high against the 185-390 ms walk-frame bake cited in the docs; difficulty days. Risk: skinning blends make distances inexact near joints (Lipschitz bound above 1), so add a safety margin.
