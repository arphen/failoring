## Idea entries from the research report (verbatim)

### K1. Compliant skin layer on the SDF, with contact pressure output
*Sources: contact note (D1, D2, D3), haptics note (Q6), garment note (fit), Godot note (ease map).*
**Outline.** Replace the hard surface at distance zero by a stiff-then-soft law: if cloth is at signed distance s < 0, the body pushes with p = k_region * penetration, up to a maximum depth (soft-tissue thickness, roughly mm to a cm by region); beyond it the surface is rigid. This is a smooth nonlinear spring energy inside the existing Newton solve, with k and max depth from a per-region stiffness map painted on the MakeHuman UV. It lets the cloth sink the planned fraction of a millimetre without a constraint fight and gives pressure in kPa for free. Extensions in the note: warp the SDF by the load-induced displacement field with a volume-preservation correction (D2, weeks, bulging has no verified source, visual only) or a quasi-static FEM flesh layer (D3, months, needs a flesh mesh and tissue parameters, none verified).
**Assessment.** Payoff high (the note ties it to the docs' plus-size squeeze). Difficulty days. Risks: no verified tissue-stiffness numbers (calibrate or expose sliders); compliance must be stiff relative to garment tension; inter-thigh flesh needs body self-collision. Dissertation-level support is thin: the contact note found no 2024-2026 paper on tight cloth on a soft body and relied on inference; skintight-clothing design work exists only as a title ([ACM DL](https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/3386569.3392477)). Pressure clinical context: apparel-engineering FEM computes clothing pressure with anisotropic fabrics, e.g. pantyhose legend 0-1.08 kPa ([Kobayashi et al. 2011](https://www.3ds.com/assets/invest/2023-10/cg-analysis-of-clothing-pressure-on-the-human-body.pdf)) and a CT-based waist model with a quadratic pressure-displacement fit ([Dan and Shi 2021](https://www.emeraldinsight.com/insight/content/doi/10.1108/IJCST-03-2020-0037/full/html)); the haptics note found **no peer-reviewed comfort thresholds**, so do not label maps "comfortable". Licence: n/a (own code). Depends on nothing; G4 consumes its pressure.
