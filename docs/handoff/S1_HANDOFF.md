### S1. Static equilibrium solve with gravity ramp and warm start (Docs: not done)
*Sources: solver note (section 3, idea 1, idea 6).*
**Outline.** Replace the 60 Hz frame loop by stages: gravity scaled from 0.05 to 1; each stage minimises elastic + gravity + a proximal term (mu/2h^2)|x - x_prev|_M^2 with the existing Newton and line search; reduce mu after each success and raise it on line-search failure (pseudo-transient continuation); stop on the acceleration-style criterion of Longva et al. rather than residual norm. After an edit, warm start from the previous solution with a short continuation from about 90 % strain, reusing the symbolic factorisation when connectivity is unchanged. End with a perturb-and-compare stability probe, and report the energy spread across 2-3 perturbed starts as a confidence cue.
**Assessment.** Payoff high; difficulty days. Risks: many Newton solves (each a Cholesky, ~0.37 s at 1500 vertices) so wall time can exceed a few dynamic seconds; thin shells under compression have multiple equilibria, so the result is a stable drape reachable from a physical loading path, not a unique one (no graphics source on bifurcation was found; the practical defence is inference). Supporting sources: *Pitfalls of Projection* (Longva et al., arXiv 2311.14526): classical Newton often beats projected Newton, Project-on-Demand Newton, acceleration-based convergence criterion, parameter-free line search ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.14526)); the optimisation-integrator view (Gast et al. 2015) already underlies Failoring ([PDF](https://www.math.ucdavis.edu/~jteran/papers/GSSJT15.pdf)). No cloth-specific 2023-26 source on homotopy, L-BFGS statics or dynamic relaxation was found. Hysteresis after edits (staying in the old buckling mode) calls for a "re-settle from scratch" button.
