# T9.1 — Calibration wizard and records (§10)

A swatch of a yarn on a hook is measured at the kitchen table, the numbers go into the wizard, and the stitch model then uses them instead
of the starter values for the parts that were measured. The crochet scene says "(calibrated)" in the status line and "Calibrated: swatch
of <date>, <yarn> on <hook> mm (gauge, weight, cantilever, stretch)" in the metrics tab instead of "UNVERIFIED starter values".

## Core (`crochet_core/include/failoring/crochet/calibration.hpp`, `src/crochet_calibration.cpp`)

`fitCalibration(SwatchMeasurements) -> CalibrationFit {Calibration cal, notes, errors, lockReachedWale/Course}`, the formulas of §10:

| step | input | result |
|---|---|---|
| gauge | stitches and rows in 10 cm | w = 0.1 / stitches, h = 0.1 / rows (m) |
| weight | mass, and the swatch size in cm or as stitch and row counts (area from the gauge) | density = mass / area |
| cantilever | overhang L of the wale strip and of the course strip (mm); needs the density | c = L / 2, B = density g c^3 (bendWale, bendCourse) |
| stretch | per direction: width, L0, W1, L1, W2, L2 | T = W g / width, e = (L - L0) / L0, k0 = T1 / e1, second slope / k0; locked at e1 when the ratio is at least 1.5 |

Steps are independent: a part without numbers stays at the starter value; impossible numbers are an error that names the step (W2 <= W1,
no rows with the stitches, no density for the cantilever, a strip shorter than L0, only one of the two strips ...) and that step is not
in the record. A strip that shows no lock gets the lock strain 1 (never reached) and a note "no lock reached, use a heavier W2"; the two
directions share one lockRatio (the mean of those that locked). The model side (`stitchDims`, `stitchMech`, the hinge map) already took
a `Calibration` since T3.1/T5.2.

## Extension (`CrochetCalibration`, and `CrochetPattern.set_calibration`)

* `CrochetCalibration.fit(measurements) -> {record, notes, errors, lock_reached_*, empty}` and `compare(yarn, hook, record)` (one sc
  stitch, starter and calibrated).
* `CrochetPattern.set_calibration(record)` / `get_calibration()`; the model, the scene build and `get_summary()` (new `calibrated`) use it.

## Records and wizard (Godot)

* `crochet_records.gd`: `user://crochet/swatches.json` (override with `FL_RECORDS`), a JSON array, one record per (yarn, hook mm, stitch).
  Record = the fitted parts (SI: w and h in m, density kg/m^2, bend N*m, k N/m, lock strains and ratio) + `{yarn, hook_mm, stitch, date,
  notes, measured}`; `measured` holds the wizard's inputs so that they come back when it is reopened; `hinge: {blo: {bendScale,
  restAngleDeg}}` is filled by the hinge fit (T9.2) and survives re-measuring the fabric.
* `crochet_calibrate.gd`: the "Calibrate" button opens it for the panel's yarn and hook (the yarn's own hook size when the panel says 0).
  Four sections with their instructions, the Peirce diagram (table edge, strip, 41.5 degree card), a live result panel (what the fit
  gives, errors in red, one sc stitch starter vs with the record, changed numbers in bold), Save and Delete record. Saving recompiles
  the pattern.
* The record applies to that yarn on that hook only (and the compare view takes the record of each piece's yarn and hook; its table says
  which pieces are calibrated).

## Evidence

* `test_crochet_calibration` (new, 18 checks): the §10 example numbers (14 sts and 16 rows in 10 cm -> w 7.143 mm, h 6.25 mm; 9 g on
  144 cm^2 -> 0.625 kg/m^2; overhangs 52 and 38 mm -> bendWale 1.08e-4, bendCourse 4.21e-5 N*m; stretch -> kWale 613.1, kCourse 245.2 N/m,
  the wale strip locks at 0.04 with ratio 3.0, the course strip reaches no lock), `stitchDims`/`stitchMech` take the measured values (sc
  7.143 x 6.250 mm vs the starter 6.5 x 6.0; density 0.625 vs 0.436; kWale 613 vs 400), the whole pouch model weighs 36.1 g with the record and
  22.1 g without with the same 2204 triangles; gauge alone, counts without a gauge, impossible numbers (4 named errors), one weight only,
  only one strip, nothing measured.
* `crochet_calibrate_check` (new, 26 PASS, 70 s): records (two hooks are two records, another yarn or hook finds nothing, re-measuring keeps
  hinge fits, save/load round trip, remove); before: not calibrated, banner UNVERIFIED; wizard opens for acrylic worsted on 5.00 mm; the
  example numbers give all four parts, the course strip's note, the result table; save writes the file; after: calibrated, yarn 39.8 -> 65.0 m,
  mass 22.1 -> 36.1 g, status "(calibrated)", banner names the parts, the run's metrics use the calibrated yarn; another hook or yarn: not
  calibrated; reopened wizard shows the inputs; compare view: the matching piece gets the record, the other not, table says which; delete:
  back to 39.8 m.
* Mutations caught: bending length c = L instead of L / 2; any second slope counts as a lock; the hook ignored in the record lookup (3
  checks); the scene build dropping the calibration (the run's yarn is no longer the calibrated one).
* Screenshot (real renderer): `docs/crochet-shots/ui/calibrate.png`, the wizard with the example numbers.

## Not done / notes

* The numbers are the plan's example numbers, not a real swatch; a real calibration needs the user's measurements.
* The hinge section of the wizard (§10.5) is T9.2.
* A record is for the stitch `sc`; other stitch types scale from it by the starter ratios (stitch_model.hpp).
