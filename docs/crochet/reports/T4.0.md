**Found during the spot-check (worked on the report's own tool, so it had to be fixed):**
`node tools/crochet/cp_export.mjs patterns/cp/<big>.txt | <consumer>` truncates at the pipe
buffer: pouch piped 131072 bytes vs 295632 through `--out`; tube40 73728 vs 105840; piped JSON
fails to parse. Cause: `process.stdout.write(json); process.exit(...)` — `process.exit` aborts
pending asynchronous pipe writes (file writes are synchronous, which is why round 1 missed it —
and the report's §8 documents stdout as the primary usage).

**Verdict round 1: changes_requested** — fix the stdout flush and prove pipe/file equality.

## Decision (recorded on the board)

**B — adopt CrochetPARADE**: `patterns/cp/*.txt` replace `.fcro`; the vendored clone
(`third_party/crochetparade` @ `06e987b13ccfc94cfdfcebc1fe4664d37d2c6687`, gitignored) is the front
end; the two things the grammar cannot express (second targets of through-both-layers stitches and
strap-end joins; optionally piece names) go into `#!` extension lines the importer reads;
`docs/CROCHET_DSL.md` will pin the syntax (T4.1). T4.1 = exporter + C++ importer into `StitchGraph`
+ extension-line reader; T4.2 = RS/WS facing, point seams from `links`, count checks. Fold and
surface-slst placement are T5.3 layout concerns. The bag layout (66 s) must run on a worker/cache
(T7.1). The tube's radius drift gets another look in T5.3.
