# T9.2 — Hinge auto-fit (§10.5)

The wizard's step 5 finds a hinge's `bendScale` from one droop measurement, by running the simulator.

## The measurement and the simulated setup

A swatch of 10 rows, the hinge row (worked BLO, FLO or as a row of sl sts) and 10 rows, 10 stitches wide. (a) Laid free on a table, right
side up, the angle the upper part settles at is the rest angle (positive toward the WS). (b) With the lower half clamped flat and the hinge at
the table edge, the angle the upper half droops below the horizontal. The simulator does the same: `makeHingeSwatch` builds the graph (the
foundation chain of 11, 21 rows of sc, the hinge row at row 11), the sheet is laid flat with the rows along +x and the right side up, the
foundation chain and rows 1-10 are pinned, gravity acts (new `RelaxOptions::gravity`), and the model relaxes. Droop = the angle below the
horizontal of the line from the centre of row 10 to the centre of row 21. The fabric record of the same yarn and hook (gauge, weight,
cantilever, stretch) is used for the swatch when there is one.

## The fit (`crochet/hinge_fit.hpp`, `crochet_hinge_fit.cpp`)

`simulateDroop(setup, yarn, bendScale, restDeg)` is one run; `fitHingeBendScale(setup, yarn, restDeg, measuredDroopDeg)` brackets with the
softest (0.01) and the stiffest (3.0) hinge, then bisects on log(bendScale) (the droop falls as the hinge stiffens) until the simulated droop
is within 0.02 deg or 14 bisections have run (usually 10-13 runs in all, each about 1 s). A measurement outside what any bendScale gives returns
the nearest end with `bracketed = false` and a note ("check the rest angle"). The droop hardly changes with bendScale for a stiff hinge (a note
says so when the result is above 0.8); the fit is best for the soft hinges that matter for bags (0.1-0.6).

**Finding:** a run that stops when the kinetic energy is below 1e-9 J (the relaxation's criterion) stopped too early here: the heavily
damped sheet creeps with a tiny KE, and the droop was not even monotone in bendScale (0.6 drooped more than 0.3). The droop run therefore
steps in chunks of 50 frames until the droop moves less than 0.005 deg over two chunks. The same early stopping could affect the other
KE-stopped runs (the bag relaxation and hang); I have not measured it there.

## Extension and wizard

* `CrochetCalibration.hinge_droop(...)` and `fit_hinge(...)`; `CrochetPattern.get_summary()` has `hinge_scale_min` / `hinge_scale_max` (the range of
  bendScale over the hinge edges) so that a hinge calibration can be seen at work.
* Wizard section 5: the construction (BLO, FLO, sl st row), the rest and droop angles (0.1 deg steps), "Fit bendScale" (a worker thread), the
  result line, and the entry goes into the record's `hinge` ({blo: {bendScale, restAngleDeg}}), merged with the entries already saved, with the
  inputs kept for reopening. A hinge-only record can be saved. The model already takes `Calibration::hinge` (T5.2).

## Evidence

* `test_crochet_hinge_fit` (new, 78 s): the swatch (22 rows, 210 sc, 31 chains, hinge row BLO / 10 sl sts); the droop falls from 87 to 31 deg
  (rest 0) and from 89 to 71.5 deg (rest 60) over bendScale 0.01 ... 3, every run settled; a ten times stiffer fabric record droops 6.4 deg
  instead of 53.6; **recovering a known bendScale from its simulated droop**: 0.10 -> 0.1001, 0.30 -> 0.2998, 0.60 -> 0.5998 (rest 0) and 0.20 ->
  0.2002 (rest 60), all within 0.1 % (bar 5 %), 11-13 runs; a stiff hinge (1.5) is fitted and the note is there; 130 deg and 5 deg are reported as
  out of range after the two end runs.
* `crochet_calibrate_check` 33 PASS (7 new): bendScale 0.10 droops 83.78 deg, the wizard fits 0.0992 (the 0.1 deg field rounds the droop);
  the entry shows in the wizard and in the saved record with the rest angle; the facing swatch's smallest hinge bendScale goes from 0.30 to the
  fitted one; the Fit button on its thread stores flo (bendScale 0.249 for 0.25, 16 s); a droop of 4 deg is reported ("No fit") and the stored
  entry stays; reopening shows the entry and the inputs.
* Mutations caught: bisection direction swapped (5 checks), gravity off (9 checks), the fit stored although out of range.
* Screenshot (real renderer): `docs/crochet-shots/ui/calibrate_hinge.png`.

## Notes

* `RelaxOptions::gravity` is new (default 0, so every other run is unchanged).
* The simulated droop is the same code as the rest of the model, so it inherits its starter assumptions; the fit makes the hinge consistent with
  the user's measurement under that model.
