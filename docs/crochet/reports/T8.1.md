# T8.1 — Chart view (§9.1)

`godot/crochet/crochet_chart.gd` is a Control that draws one symbol per stitch and sits in the Chart tab; `crochet_main.gd` feeds it
`pattern.get_chart()` after every compile.

* **Symbols** (US): ch oval, sl st dot, sc "x", hdc "T", dc "T" with one slash, tr two slashes. BLO is an arc under the symbol, FLO an
  arc over it, "space" a box. RS stitches are light, WS stitches blue; a stitch that carries a join is orange, a both-layers seam
  pink. An increase or a decrease (a base worked into twice, or a stitch worked into two bases) gets a thin leg to each base. Rows
  are faint lines through their stitches in made order (straight for flat work, rings for rounds). Below 8 px per stitch it falls
  back to one dot per stitch.
* **Hover** reads out "stitch 100: row 6 #5, sc, WS, line 13" in the corner; **click** selects (yellow ring) and emits
  `stitch_selected(id)`; **wheel** zooms about the pointer, **right/middle drag** pans, **double-click** fits. `focus(id, zoom)`
  centres a stitch (T8.2 will use it). Only symbols in view are drawn.
* **Placement** is `fl::chartPositions` (core, `crochet_layout.cpp`), not the 3D pose:
  * flat pieces as they were worked, with the fold opened out (`flatPosition`, new `LayoutReport::foldY`): the pouch body is 50
    rows, not 25 rows drawn twice; the foundation chain goes with the piece it starts;
  * the rounds of a revolve piece are concentric rings seen from above; the walls rise straight up, so each wall stitch is pushed out
    along the outline's normal by its height, which unrolls the profile (the bag is an oval of 44 rings);
  * strap and rim rows by (index, row), moved right of the main piece only when they would lie on it (not for the pouch or the
    bag; tested with a 3x scaled bag model);
  * one chart unit is the median non-chain stitch (the first version took stitch 6, a chain, so body rows were twice the strap's).
* `stitchUv` (T7.4) now uses the same opened-out position, so the relief on the back layer of a folded piece runs the same way as the
  front.

## Evidence

* `crochet_scene_check`: 38 PASS (12 new): 1380 symbols for the pouch, all drawable types and finite, row 1 below row 5, the folded
  body is 49 units tall (48.9) and the border the next row up (1.1), a point on stitch 100 hits stitch 100, hover text with the line,
  that line of the pattern is the stitch's own row, WS/RS colours, click selects and signals, wheel zoom keeps the stitch under
  the pointer, fit resets, a new pattern replaces the chart.
* `test_crochet_layout`: pouch unfolded (750 stitches, rows in order, nearest pair 6.0 mm of a 5.5 mm stitch); pouch chart (foundation
  1.0 below row 1, border 1.1 above row 50); bag chart (rounds 1-44 are rings growing to 45.6 units, nearest pair 0.88 units, strap
  clear); 3x bag (strap moved right, 270 > 266).
* Mutations caught: skipping every 100th stitch (5 checks), wrong line number (after the check was tightened: the first version
  of the check let it through), fold left closed, no unrolling, no strap shift (after the 3x test was added: on the real bag the
  strap is clear anyway, so that mutant was equivalent until then).
* Screenshots (real renderer): `docs/crochet-shots/ui/chart_pouch.png`, `chart_bag.png`, `chart_pouch_zoom.png`.
