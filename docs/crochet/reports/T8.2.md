# T8.2 — Selection: chart <-> 3D <-> pattern line (§9.1)

One selection (`selection`, the stitch ids) with three views, in `crochet_main.gd`:

* **Chart**: yellow rings on every selected stitch (`CrochetChart.set_highlight`), the read-out line says what it is ("stitch 100:
  row 6 #5, sc, WS, line 13", or "16 stitches, line 13"); `reveal(id)` scrolls a selected stitch into view.
* **Cloth**: the selected stitches' vertices get vertex colour alpha 0 (`highlight_colours`); `crochet_surface.gdshader` turns that
  into a yellow glow (`varying hl`). The mesh is rebuilt on a selection change when no run is going.
* **Editor**: the caret goes to the stitch's pattern line and the line is selected. The other way round, a caret on a line selects
  all stitches of that line (chart rings and glow follow). The caret move we make ourselves comes back as a deferred `caret_changed`;
  `_synced_line` stops it from turning a one-stitch selection into the whole line.

Ways in: a click in the chart; a click on the cloth (`pick_at` casts the camera ray through the point, `pick_ray` tests it against the
mid-surface triangles with `Geometry3D.ray_intersects_triangle` and takes the nearest vertex of the nearest triangle; a left click that
moves under 4 px, so camera drags do not select); the caret. A click on nothing clears the selection.

## Evidence

* `crochet_scene_check`: 51 PASS (13 new).
  * Chart/editor: a chart click selects stitch 100 and puts the caret on line 13 with `sc.R6` selected; the deferred caret event does
    not widen it; caret on line 10 selects its 16 stitches, on line 13 its 16 (chart rings and read-out agree); a comment line
    clears it; a click on empty chart clears it.
  * Cloth: stitch 100 maps to vertex 94 and back; alpha 0 on exactly that vertex and colours otherwise unchanged; a line lights 15 of
    its 16 stitches (the turning chain has no vertex); a ray from the front through a row 3 vertex returns that stitch, a ray that
    misses returns none; a click on the camera's projection of that vertex selects it and moves the caret to its line; a click beside
    the cloth clears it.
* Mutations caught: the own-caret-move guard removed (1 check), nearest vertex replaced by the farthest (2 checks).
* Screenshot (real renderer): `docs/crochet-shots/ui/select_line.png`, line 30 of the pouch selected: a yellow band on the cloth near
  the fold and the matching row of rings in the chart.
