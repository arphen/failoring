# T5.4 — Relaxation + the §6.10 geometry tests (circle flat / cup / ruffle, swatch, tube, hinge sign)

## What was built

* `crochet/relax.hpp`, `crochet_relax.cpp` (new): `makeSolver(model, params)` (the model as one intrinsic
  panel + its point seams; T6.1's scenes reuse it) and `relaxModel(model, RelaxOptions)`: zero gravity, no
  colliders, seams fading in over 60 frames, self collision on, damping x5, step until KE < 1e-9 J (after the
  ramp) or 400 frames; `noise` (deterministic start perturbation), `pin`, `newtonIterations` (30), diagnostics
  (frames, KE, seam gap, Newton cap hits).
* `test_crochet_relax.cpp` (new): §6.10 tests 1, 2, 3, 5 and the pouch relaxation. ~60 s. With
  `FL_RELAX_SHOTS=<dir>` it writes PNGs of every relaxed shape (docs/crochet-shots/relax/).
* One more test in `test_crochet_mesh.cpp` (chain-edge rest area).

## Three changes the tests forced (all recorded where the constant lives)

1. **`compressionRatio` 0.2 -> 0.1** (stitch_model.cpp). At the model's w/h = 1.083 a +6 sc circle has
   about 3.4 % more rim than its radial stitch heights allow (a flat disc needs the increase wedge angle
   2 asin(w/2h) = 60 deg, i.e. w = h). With the plan's 0.2 the sheet buckles to a ruffle (+-10 % of the radius)
   from any 0.3 mm perturbation, so test 2 "flat < 5 %" fails. Sweep (noise 0.3 mm, 3 seeds, noise 0.05-3 mm):
   0.07-0.15 keeps +6 flat (0.0 %) every time, +5 a 43 % cup, +8 a ~74 % ruffle; 0.2 fails; below 0.05 the +5
   cup collapses. 0.1 is in the middle of the working band. UNVERIFIED like all of §4.4 (calibration §10 replaces it).
2. **Foundation chain edges are stretched to the width of the stitch worked into them** (crochet_mesh.cpp):
   a `ch` is 0.85 w_sc, so row 1 above a chain was a trapezoid with a short base and every oval base curled into a
   keel. Now the row-1 polygons are w x h rectangles (facing swatch row 1: 156.00 mm^2 = 4 w h). T5.1's hygiene
   numbers are unchanged.
3. **Newton budget 30** in the relaxation (default 12): at 12 the pouch hit the cap in 82 % of its substeps
   (319/388); at 30 there are 0/380 and the same shape (plan 1 rule 6: a capped run is not a result).

## Honest notes

* The +8 "ruffle" is a two-wave saddle (4 sign changes, the minimum the plan allows), amplitude +-33 mm,
  identical for every seed and perturbation size, so the criterion is met but not by a margin on the count.
* The bag relaxes in 175 frames (180 s on this machine, 5654 vertices, seam gap 17 um, 1 cap hit in 350
  substeps) but is not in the test: ~3 min is too slow for ctest; T6.1 runs it as a scene.
* The tube/bag base is still a narrow oval (r_1 = h) with walls on it, so a tube without load opens into an
  elliptical bowl; a bag with a laptop inside is the T6.2 question.
