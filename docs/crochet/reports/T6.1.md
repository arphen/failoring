# T6.1 — Scenes `hang_pin`, `hang_peg`, settle loop (§7.2, §7.4 test 3 for the pouch)

## What was built

* `crochet/scene.hpp`, `crochet_scene.cpp` (new): `CrochetScene(model, graph, layout, SceneOptions)` orients a
  (relaxed) model hanging by its strap, builds the solver (`makeSolver`, gravity on, `selfThickness = min(0.8 t,
  0.4 min(w, h))`, Newton budget 30) and `settle()` steps until the kinetic energy is below 1e-8 J for 30
  frames in a row or the frame cap (1500); it reports frames, KE, Newton iterations and cap hits, the exact
  self-intersection count and the clearance to the peg. The decisions are the header comment of `scene.hpp`.
* `test_crochet_scene.cpp` (new): relaxes the pouch, then the orientation test, hang_pin and hang_peg with the
  plan's §7.4 test 3 checks. With `FL_SCENE_SHOTS=<dir>` it writes PNGs of both hung pouches.

## Decisions

* **Up** = from the centroid of everything that is not strap to the middle of the strap (what the object hangs
  from); the assembly is rotated to put it on +y and translated so the middle of the strap is over the origin in x
  and z. Tested by rotating the relaxed pouch by 0.9 rad about z and -0.6 rad about x first.
* **hang_pin** pins every vertex of the two middle rows of the strap.
* **hang_peg**: the capsule (radius 1.2 cm, 10 cm long, along x) is placed INSIDE the strap loop, 2 cm below the
  loop's inner apex. The plan says "2 cm above the strap's top", but a strap can only hang from a peg that is
  inside its loop; the 2 cm becomes the drop before the strap catches. `bodyFrictionMode = 2` as in the plan.

## Measured (Release, pouch, acrylic worsted 5 mm)

| scene | frames to settle | KE at the end | Newton cap hits | self intersections | peg clearance | hang (highest to lowest point) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| hang_pin | 239 | 5.4e-9 J | 0 of 478 substeps | 0 | – | 38.8 cm (strap 24 + body 15) |
| hang_peg | 276 | 4.8e-9 J | 0 of 552 substeps | 0 | +3.88 mm | 38.6 cm |

* The pinned rows did not move (0.0 m). The tilted model ends up hung by the strap with the body 25.2 cm below the
  pins, the pins at x = -7.6e-18, z = 1.5e-17 m.
* The hung pouch stays centred on the peg: centroid x = 0.0, z = -0.1 cm.
* Relaxation of the pouch before the scenes: 192 frames, seam gap 0.580 mm. The test takes ~3.5 min.
