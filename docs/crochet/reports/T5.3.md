## Approach (all inferred from the graph, no text directives)

Rows are grouped into regions in made order: Foundation / Flat (turned rows off the foundation) /
Revolve (closed rounds off the foundation) / Rim (an open round: border, strap-end join) / Strip (turned
rows off a Revolve or Rim region: the strap). Flat hangs in the xy plane (foundation on top, wale down,
RS toward +z) with the plan's tridiagonal least squares per row; Revolve rises from a base in the xz plane
with the plan's rule r_k = (C_k - 2L)/2pi, y_k = y_{k-1} + sqrt(max(h^2 - dr^2, 0)) on the offset curve
of the centre path (the oval is a stadium; its first ring has r_1 = h, so the legs have the stitch height);
the fold comes from the `both_layers` links (constant row sum 51 -> between rows 25 and 26); the Rim goes one
stitch height outside the boundary of what exists; the Strip is a flat strip embedded on a circular arc
through the chord to the `join` targets, bulging up, of the right length. Native layout, not
CrochetPARADE's positions (plan option A/B): the spike measured 66 s for the bag and a drifting tube.

## Honest notes / risks handed to T5.4

* The layout is only a guess: edge lengths of the bag reach 0.36 and 2.34 of rest at the oval base caps
  and the strap start. The relaxation decides.
* The oval's foundation chain has rest edge `0.85 w` while the stitches worked into it are `1.0 w`: the
  first rounds of a bag/tube are trapezoids and may ruffle along the spine. Treated in T5.4 if the
  relaxation shows it (e.g. use the worked stitch width as the chain edge's rest length).
* The strap leaves the rim tangent to the chord (the symmetric arc), i.e. horizontally for the
  pouch, not straight up; physically plausible loop, but its first rows sit across the mouth.
