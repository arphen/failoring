## Decisions worth knowing

* **Mass.** The plan's nominal density `yarnLen*lambda/(w*h)` loses mass wherever a stitch's
  triangles cover less than w*h (an increase triangle is half a stitch: 6 per round of a +6 circle),
  so the 0.5 % test cannot pass with it. Each stitch's triangles get density `M_s / sum(area)` instead
  (increase triangle: 1.038 kg/m^2 vs nominal 0.436); a stitch that owns no triangle (first stitch
  of a row, dropped nodes) lumps its yarn into `extraMass` on its own / its `dropTo` vertex.
  Materials are deduplicated on (type, insertion, density to 1e-6 kg/m^2, surface stiffening), so
  fixtures end up with 2-6 materials.
* **Sign into the solver.** Mesh normals point to the RS; a positive solver rest angle bends toward
  the common normal side (BendC::rest). The plan's "positive = toward the WS" is therefore a
  NEGATIVE solver angle: `kWsFoldSign = -1`. This is derived from the T1.3 convention and the
  orientation of T5.1; the simulation test of plan 6.10 test 5 (T5.4, 25 +- 3 deg toward the WS) is
  the empirical check, and flips this constant if it measures the opposite.
* **slst join rows get the sl st firmness** (1.5 on base and top edges): the pouch's and the bags'
  strap-end join rows are sl st rows with faces, so the pouch has 8 and each bag 10 such modifiers
  even in the `plain` variants. Closing sl sts of rounds have no faces and no hinges.
* A `blo` sl st round multiplies: base edges 0.30 x 1.5 with the blo angle, top edges 1.5.
* Lock terms are mapped here (`lockStrainWarp = lockWale`, `lockStrainWeft = lockCourse`,
  `lockStiffnessRatio`), as the board decision of 2026-10-16 said; `toFabric` is unchanged.
